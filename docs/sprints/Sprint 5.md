# Sprint 5

Harbor season launch: check-in on the pontoon

## Retrospective

Two dockhands at Vik checked boats in from the pontoon all weekend on their own phones, in the rain, with one bar of signal. Check-in, the QR sticker on the cleat (`HARBOR-143`) that opens the right berth's booking, and the photograph of the boat's papers (`HARBOR-144`) all shipped, and the whole check-in epic is finished. Ola had both phones on the build a week before the launch instead of on the Friday, which is the only reason the weekend was boring.

Two things we would rather had gone differently. The old test phone's clock is eleven minutes fast and queued check-ins carried it (`HARBOR-153`), so the office saw arrivals out of order and twice in the future — found on the Thursday, fixed by the Tuesday, but it is the kind of thing a launch weekend should not be discovering. And season pricing (`HARBOR-142`) has been sitting in review since the middle of the sprint waiting for us to decide what happens when a season berth is cut short, which is a product question that a card in review cannot answer on its own.

The kiosk (`HARBOR-121`) was cancelled during the sprint rather than carried: it is the same job as the sticker, and saying so cost one conversation with Sandholm. We should do that earlier and more often. Next sprint starts by taking a decision on the shortened-season refund rather than by pulling more work in beside it.
