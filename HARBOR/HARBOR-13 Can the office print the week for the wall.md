## What was asked

"The screen is fine but the girl on the desk at the weekend is seventeen and she is not going to have a laptop open on the pontoon. We printed the week off the old planner every Friday and it went on the wall by the kettle. Can it print?"

## What they are trying to do

Hand the weekend over to somebody who is not going to be trained on anything. The paper is not nostalgia — it is the one artefact in the harbour office that works when the wifi does not and that four people can stand in front of at once.

## What we did about it

Yes, and it took until August to write down because we kept treating it as a print stylesheet. It is a view: a week, every berth, who is arriving and leaving, on one sheet of A3. It is on the board and it is not scheduled.
