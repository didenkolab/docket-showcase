The season is June to September and it happens once. A marina that tries Harbor in July and goes back to the wall planner in August has told us something no survey will: that the software was harder than the paper it replaced, on the week it mattered. So the objective is not adoption in the abstract. It is four marinas taking the whole of their season through Harbor and not keeping a second copy of the truth beside it.
