## What was asked

"For a season berth nobody wants to put eighteen thousand on a card in March. We have always taken a third up front and the rest at Easter. If Harbor cannot do that I will be taking season money on the phone again next year."

## What they are trying to do

Keep the arrangement that gets a season berth sold in March, when the boat is under a tarpaulin and the owner is not feeling wealthy.

## What we did about it

Not yet, and it is the request most likely to be the next thing we build. There is a story for taking a deposit now and the rest on arrival, and it is unsized because the question underneath it is not technical: what happens to the second payment when the season is cut short, which is the same question that has held the season-rate story in review since the middle of August.
