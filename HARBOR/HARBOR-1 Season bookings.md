A marina sells the same forty berths every year and keeps the truth of who has which one in a wall planner. Harbor's first job is to hold that truth: a guest or the harbour office reserves a berth for a range of dates, the marina invoices for it, and nobody has to ring anyone to find out whether a slip is free.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A guest can reserve a berth for a date range and get an invoice for it
- [ ] Two people cannot hold the same berth for the same night
- [ ] The harbour office can cancel a booking and see the berth come back
