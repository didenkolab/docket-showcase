## What could happen

The launch weekend arrives and nobody who can restore the database is reachable.

## Why we think so

Six people, no rota, and a launch on a Saturday in August when four of them have already said they are away. The restore has been done once, by one person, from memory, on a laptop. Nothing about that is unusual for a team this size and all of it is fine until the weekend it is not.

## What we would do

A runbook that says who is on call, how the marina reaches us out of hours, and a restore that somebody other than its author has rehearsed.
