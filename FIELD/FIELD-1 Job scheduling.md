A heating contractor's day is a whiteboard in the office and four phone calls to find out whether it is still true. Everything under this epic replaces the whiteboard: a job has a customer, an address, a crew and a time, the crew sees the same day the office sees, and moving a job is one action rather than four conversations.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A crew opens the app and sees the day it is actually going to have
- [ ] The office can put a job on a crew and the crew knows within the minute
- [ ] A job carries what happened last time somebody went to that address
