Nordic Field's crews work in valleys where there is no signal for hours at a time, and every scheduling tool they have tried assumed a connection and quietly lost the afternoon. The whole product rests on one claim: take the phone into the mountains, do a day's work, come back, and everything you did is there. Nothing else we build for them matters if that is not true.
