Nordic Field's crews carry a pad of job sheets the customer signs, and the office types them up again a week later from the copy that came back in the van. A signature on the phone is the same evidence without the second typing — as long as it is attached to the job the same way a photograph is, and comes back from a day offline just as reliably.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A customer signs on the phone with no signal and the job carries the signature
- [ ] The signature reaches the office with the job, not separately
- [ ] A job that was signed cannot be quietly edited afterwards without saying so
